How to play

Move: left thumb joystick (anywhere on the left side). Look: drag on the right side.

Place a block: quick tap on the right side, or the PLACE button. Break: press and hold still on the right side, or hold BREAK. The crosshair picks the block.

Jump: JUMP button (you also auto-jump up single steps). Tap a door to open/close it. Tap a bed to set your spawn (and sleep at night).

Hotbar: tap a slot to select. Blocks button opens the block picker. Mining ores earns coins; spend them in the Shop (pause menu) on blocks and faster pickaxes.

Depth: grass → stone and caves → the Deep Dark (y<72, diamonds and glowing Gloomcrystals) → Hell (y<40: Brimrock, Hellstone, lava lakes, imps) → unbreakable bedrock at y=0.

Zombies come out at night on the surface. Lava hurts unless God Mode is on. Your world autosaves every 30 seconds and when you pause or leave.

Cities (new in 1.2): towns with a market, church, farms and a town hall are scattered over the land, one just north of where a new world starts. The HUD shows the nearest city and an arrow pointing to it. Tap townspeople to chat, tap the Merchant to buy and sell blocks, and tap Chief Stevo in the town hall for a gift and a quest. Demon cities lie deep in Hell, behind lava moats. Their citizens are peaceful until you hit one, and the guards at the fortress attack on sight.

Animals (new in 1.3): cows, pigs, sheep, chickens, horses, wolves and cats roam the grass; polar bears, arctic foxes and wolves live in the snowlands. Hit livestock for raw meat (and wool). Fish swim in schools in lakes and the sea: tap one to grab it, or use a Fishing Net or Fishing Rod (cast, wait for the bite, tap again).

Food: tap a Campfire or Furnace to cook raw meat and fish. Select food and press EAT (or hold on the right side) to heal. Cooked food heals much more. A simple hunger bar can be turned on in Settings (it is off in God Mode).

Pets: hold an animal's favorite food and tap the animal a few times (dogs/wolves: meat or bones, cats: fish, cows/sheep/horses/chickens: wheat, pigs: wheat or melon, polar bears: fish, foxes: chicken or fish). When the hearts are full it is your pet: it gets a collar and a name tag, follows you, teleports to you when you are far away, and wolves fight monsters with you. Tap your pet to make it sit or follow. Rename pets in Pause → Pets.

Gear: buy swords, a battle axe, a spear, a bow with arrows and leather, iron, diamond or Emberite armor in the Shop. Hold a weapon to hit harder; open Gear to wear armor (it blocks up to 80% of damage). Buckets scoop up and pour water and lava; lava spreads a few blocks and stops, and water + lava turns into obsidian or cobblestone.

$ CHEATS are on the title screen and pause menu. They include Teleport to nearest City, Teleport to Demon City, Teleport to Snow Biome, Spawn Pet, All Gear and Full Food.
